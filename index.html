About

I'm a Shopify web developer and app developer based in Idukki, Kerala. On the storefront side I work with Liquid, theme customization and store setup. On the app side I build with Shopify Functions, theme app extensions, and merchant admins in React Router + Polaris backed by Prisma and PostgreSQL. I also work on e-commerce automation and Meta Ads.

I co-founded Qeron in September 2025. I built our Shopify store and run its Meta Ads, and I've also built custom Shopify apps that span the embedded admin, the storefront and checkout.

In April 2026 I co-founded G2G Agency. I'm open to remote work and volunteer causes including education, environmental sustainability and arts and culture.
